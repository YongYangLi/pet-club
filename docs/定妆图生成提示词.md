# 定妆图生成提示词

基准画风见 `素材/定妆图/p1.png`、`p3.png`、`p5.png`：真人照片级的写实头部 +
约 2.6~2.8 头身的 Q 版玩偶身体，照片级 3D 渲染，浅灰米白无缝影棚渐变背景 + 地面淡接触阴影，
均匀柔光，全身正面站立、头顶到脚底完整入画。

**用法**：图生图优先，把真人自拍作为第 1 张参考图，参考图保真度拉到最高。
`[ ]` 里是要替换的槽位。纯洋红 `#FF00FF` 背景只适合自动抠图，本项目不用。

---

## 一、主提示词（中文，国内生图工具用）

```text
参考图中的真人，做一张全身「大头娃娃」3D 定妆照：脸部、五官、[眼镜]、[发型]、[耳钉]、肤色完全按照片还原，照片级写实质感，焦点锐利，发丝和皮肤纹理清晰。身体是 Q 版幼儿比例，头算 1 份、下巴到脚底约 1.7 份（约 2.7 头身），肩宽圆、躯干圆润饱满、四肢短粗、手脚小而圆；不要成人比例、不要瘦长身体、不要细腿窄肩。全身正面站立，略带自然重心偏移，人物居中，头顶和双脚完整入画、不裁切。穿 [上衣]、[下装]、[鞋子]，衣料褶皱与材质写实。表情自然放松：眼睛睁开有神、嘴角轻抬、嘴唇闭合或只露一线上门牙、脸颊微鼓、左右略不对称；不要咧嘴大笑、不要露整排牙、不要眯成月牙、不要摆拍假笑。背景是浅灰米白无缝影棚，柔和径向渐变，地面有淡淡接触阴影，均匀柔光，高清 3D 渲染，无柔焦、无磨皮、无塑料感。不要文字、边框、拼贴、logo、水印，不要洋红纯色背景，不要多个人物，不要半身构图。
```

## 二、主提示词（英文，推荐给支持图生图的工具）

```text
Create a full-body "big-head" 3D character portrait based on the person in the reference image.

HEAD: keep it photographic and faithful to the photo - identical face and features, [GLASSES], [HAIRCUT], [EAR STUD], the same skin tone and the same relaxed natural expression. Razor sharp focus, crisp clear eyes, sharp defined glasses frame edges, fine detailed skin pores and individual hair strands. No soft focus, no blur, no smoothing, no beauty filter, no waxy skin.

BODY: a stylized chibi toddler body, about 2.7 heads tall - the head is 1 unit and the chin-to-soles distance is only 1.7 units. Wide rounded shoulders, plump rounded torso, short thick arms and legs, small round hands and feet. NOT adult proportions, NOT a tall slim body, NOT thin legs, NOT narrow shoulders.

FRAMING: full-length front view, standing with a slight natural weight shift, centred in frame, the top of the head and both shoes fully visible, no cropping, no half-body shot.

OUTFIT: [TOP], [TROUSERS], [SHOES], with realistic fabric folds and materials.

EXPRESSION: the eyes stay OPEN and bright with a warm sparkle, lips closed or only a tiny sliver of the upper front teeth visible, corners of the mouth gently lifted, cheeks slightly raised, a slight natural asymmetry. NOT a stretched posed grin, NOT a full row of teeth, NOT squinting crescent eyes, NOT a stiff straight face.

SCENE: seamless light warm-grey / off-white studio backdrop with a soft radial gradient and a faint contact shadow on the floor, even soft studio lighting, high-end photorealistic 3D render, clean sharp edges.

no text, no border, no collage, no logo, no watermark, no solid magenta background, no multiple characters
```

### 槽位填法

| 槽位 | p1~p6 的实际写法 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 眼镜 | `thin transparent / clear acetate frames`、`thick black round frames` | 必须写死框型与颜色，模型很容易换框 |
| 发型 | `very short dense flat black crop, 5 mm, close to the skull, soft rounded top, short faded sides, no spikes` | 写 `spiky`、`standing upright` 一定出刺猬头 |
| 上衣 | `plain [色] crew-neck short-sleeve T-shirt`、`cream-white oversized hoodie with a kangaroo pocket` | 单件上衣最好，桌面 210px 下能读出来 |
| 下装 | `khaki cargo trousers with rolled cuffs`、`olive cargo pants`、`dark blue straight jeans` | 深色/中性色优于米色系，缩到 210px 不会和上衣糊在一起 |
| 鞋子 | `off-white chunky sneakers`、`white thick-sole canvas shoes` | 厚底 + 大脚是 Q 感的主要来源 |
| 背景 | 见上，固定浅灰米白 | 别换成洋红纯色，会和现有 5 个角色不同族 |

## 三、三个场景的追加句

**A. 从真人自拍出第一张定妆**：用第二节整段，`image1` = 真人自拍，1024×1536 竖版。
若工具只支持单参考图，比例就靠 `head is 1 unit / chin-to-soles 1.7 units` 这句文字推。

**B. 在已有定妆上改体型或换下装**（p6 F1~F3 用这套，验证有效）：
`image1` = 当前定妆图，`image2` = `素材/定妆图/p3.png`（只借头身比与圆润体型），
三张变体都从 `image1` 直接派生一次、不要串联：

```text
Keep EVERYTHING from image 1 - face, glasses, haircut, ear stud, expression, top, rendering and backdrop - EXACTLY as it is. Change ONLY the trousers and shoes / the body proportions.
Use image 2 ONLY as a reference for the chibi body proportions and rounded silhouette. Do NOT copy its face, glasses, hairstyle, clothing, watch or background.
The head is 1 unit, the chin-to-soles distance is 1.65 units: wide rounded shoulders, plump rounded torso, short thick arms and legs, small round hands and feet.
[TROUSERS: light khaki cargo trousers with rolled cuffs / dark blue straight jeans / charcoal cargo shorts with white socks] and [SHOES].
no text, no border, no collage, no logo
```

**C. 从定妆派生动作图**（定妆选定后再用）：

```text
Same character as the reference image: identical face, hair, glasses, outfit, rendering, head-to-body proportion and backdrop. Change ONLY the pose: [ONE POSE SENTENCE]. no text, no border, no collage, no logo
```

## 五、参数与验收

| 项 | 值 |
|---|---|
| 画幅 | 1024×1536 竖版（站姿）；动作图里的 sleep / crawl 用 1536×1024 横版 |
| 参考图保真度 | 拉到最高（ImageGen 是 `input_fidelity: high`），关闭自动改写提示词 |
| 质量 | high |
| 头身比 | 2.6~2.9 头（body_h / head_h），头宽 / 图高 0.30~0.36 |
| 桌面验收 | 缩到 210px 高，上半身和下半身仍然能分开读出来 |
| 抠图 | 浅灰渐变影棚 + 接触阴影自动泛洪会啃衣服，抠图要人工做；右下角若带水印一并在这一步去掉 |

## 六、已验证的坑

- **别写 `2.8 heads tall`**：模型会把头画得更大（实测 1.66 头）。改写成 `head is 1 unit, chin to soles is 1.8 units`。
- **别写 `spiky / standing upright`**：短寸会变刺猬。写 `5 mm flat crop, close to the skull, no spikes`；头发要单独一轮只改头发才收得住。
- **笑容别堆词**：`sweet`、`delighted`、`eyes squeezed into crescents`、`upper teeth showing` 都出假笑。自然笑 = 眼睛睁开有光 + 嘴角轻抬 + 只露一点上牙 + 轻微不对称。
- **脸别写「可爱」**：只在体型段写圆润，脸部写可爱会放大眼睛、丢掉本人辨识度。
- **不要链式重绘**：每版都从同一张原始定妆直接派生一次，K1→M1→P4→试做1 这种链会让脸逐级发虚。
- **双参考图必须写排除句**：`Do NOT copy its face, glasses, hairstyle, clothing, watch or background`，否则第二张的脸会被混进来。
- **动作姿势别只靠文字**：`hanging in the air / feet off the ground` 无效，模型照站姿画；要拿一张已经离地的图当参考图。
- 工具若输出右下角水印，去背时一并擦掉，不要留到素材里。

## 七、换工具时的调整

- 只支持单参考图：删掉 B 段的 `image 2` 句，把体型基准全部写进文字（第二节 BODY 段已经够用）。
- 只支持文生图：删掉 `based on the person in the reference image`，把人物特征全部写全；角色一致性靠固定种子 + 风格参考（若工具提供）。
- 有独立负面词输入框：把各段里的 `NOT ...` 和结尾的 `no text, no border...` 挪进去，主提示词只留正向描述。
- 用画幅参数锁竖版（例如 2:3），否则容易出半身像。
- 出图后按第五节量一次头身比再挑，不要只看大图好不好看。
